# Brand Direction

ArenaWealth uses a restrained analytical identity. The product surface should
feel like a financial workbench, not a marketing page.

## Visual Tokens

- Space and carbon surfaces for authority and focus.
- Off-white work surfaces for dense reading.
- Gold for priority and durable-value accents.
- Blue for information and data readiness.
- Green for success, health, and completed checks.

## Product Rules

- Keep the app quiet enough for repeated analytical use.
- Use color as signal, not decoration.
- Do not copy private brand studies or domain-specific copy into the artifact.
- Keep pitch materials more expressive than the operating workbench.

## Current Application

The workbench uses the Arena palette through CSS tokens. The ignored
`brandArena/` studies remain reference material only.

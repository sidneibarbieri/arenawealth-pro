# Paper Target

The most coherent computing-and-finance target for this artifact is ACM ICAIF:
the ACM International Conference on AI in Finance. The official site describes
ICAIF as a peer-reviewed ACM conference focused on AI, machine learning, and
finance.

Venue/prize reality:

- ICAIF is the closest ACM-style venue fit for an AI finance tool artifact.
- ACM tracks best-paper awards across conferences, but the public ACM/ICAIF
  pages do not establish a cash prize for ICAIF.
- If cash prize is the primary criterion, European Finance Association awards
  list monetary prizes, but that route requires a stronger finance contribution
  and normally a finance-paper framing rather than a software artifact.

## Current Scaffold

The LaTeX scaffold under `paper/acm-icaif/` uses ACM `acmart` with the `sigconf`
format, which ACM identifies as the common proceedings format for most ACM
conference authors.

## Submission Position

The paper is not submission-ready. The missing core is empirical evidence:
backtests, baselines, ablations, turnover/cost modeling, and provider
data-quality analysis.

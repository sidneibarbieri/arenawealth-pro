# Paper and Prize Target

This file is a strategy note. It is not part of the reviewer artifact.

## Current Decision

Use a two-track strategy:

1. Paper track: ACM ICAIF is the best fit for a peer-reviewed paper about
   deterministic AI/computing for finance.
2. Product/prize track: FinTech World Cup at Dubai FinTech Summit is the better
   fit for startup visibility and prize-oriented pitching.

The research artifact should not mention the target event. The pitch deck can
use the product story, brand, and customer workflow.

## Evidence From Current Public Pages

- ICAIF 2026 is a peer-reviewed ACM conference for AI, machine learning, and
  finance. The call lists an August 2, 2026 paper deadline, an eight-page ACM
  `sigconf` format limit, and Milan, November 14-17, 2026 as the conference.
- ICAIF 2025 publicly listed a Best Paper Award prize of USD 3,000 and Best
  Student Paper prize of USD 2,000.
- FinTech World Cup is a startup competition whose grand finale is hosted during
  Dubai FinTech Summit. Public pages emphasize startup elevation; prize terms
  need confirmation before claiming an amount.

## Ranking For This Project

| Target | Best use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACM ICAIF | Paper acceptance and best-paper attempt | Strongest fit for AI/computing plus finance; requires rigorous empirical evidence |
| FinTech World Cup | Product pitch and commercial prize path | Stronger fit for a working fintech product and branding story |
| Dubai FinTech Summit | Visibility and partnerships | Summit platform around the startup competition, not primarily a paper venue |

## Current Scaffold

## Submission Position

The paper is not submission-ready. The missing core is empirical evidence:
point-in-time selection backtests, factor baselines, ablations, turnover/cost
modeling, and provider data-quality analysis.

# Market Sizing

## Frame

ArenaWealth targets self-directed equity investors who manage their own
portfolios with free online tools but lack structured decision support,
data provenance, and audit trails.

---

## TAM — Total Addressable Market

**Retail investment management software globally**

- Global retail wealth management AUM: ~$80 trillion (2024, BCG Global Wealth Report)
- Digital investment platforms and tools: growing ~12% per year
- Market for retail investor decision-support software: estimated $5-8B by 2027

The TAM is large but diffuse. We do not compete with full-service wealth
management platforms.

---

## SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market

**Self-directed equity investors using digital tools**

- US self-directed brokerage accounts: ~160 million (SIFMA, 2023)
- International retail investors using US equities: growing, Brazil alone has
  500k+ active investors on US-focused brokers (B3 and CVM reports, 2024)
- Independent advisors (RIAs) in the US: ~17,000 registered (SEC, 2023)
- Estimated annual spend on decision-support tools per advisor: $2,000-5,000

SAM estimate: $1.2-2.5B (US self-directed investors + international broker users
  + independent advisor tooling)

---

## SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market (Year 1-2)

**International equity holders using Avenue, Interactive Brokers, or similar
  brokers who export CSV portfolios**

- Avenue (Brazilian retail US broker): ~500k+ registered accounts
- Similar Latin American brokers with US equity access: growing 20-30% per year
- Target: users who download CSV exports and currently use spreadsheets
- Conversion assumption: 1-2% of ~500k users at $0-10/month freemium tier

SOM estimate (year 1): 5,000-10,000 users; $0 revenue (acquisition) → $50-100k
  ARR at freemium-to-paid conversion

---

## Growth Thesis

1. Start with Brazilian international equity investors (high CSV export
   behavior, low tool quality, Avenue partnership potential).
2. Expand to independent advisors with a multi-portfolio workspace and audit log.
3. Phase 3: licensed platform for personalized recommendation (requires RIA
   or equivalent).

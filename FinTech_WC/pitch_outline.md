# Pitch Outline

## 1. Opening

Portfolio recommendations should be auditable. Today, many investor tools show
outputs without making the data path, provider health, and historical evidence
easy to inspect.

## 2. Product

ArenaWealth Pro is a deterministic portfolio workbench for long-term equity
holders and research-driven advisors. It proposes orders, ranks holdings, screens
external candidates, and records the evidence needed to replay decisions.

## 3. Why Now

Free financial data sources are broad enough to build useful workflows, but they
are uneven. Users need software that shows what is working, what is missing, and
which claims are supported.

## 4. Differentiation

- Deterministic policy engine.
- Provider readiness and live health checks.
- Snapshot and replay path.
- Free-data-first architecture.
- Honest performance boundaries.

## 5. Demo

- Source health.
- Portfolio dashboard.
- Cash-deployment recommendation.
- Candidate universe screen.
- Price baseline study.

## 6. Ask

Use the competition to validate advisor workflows, meet data and distribution
partners, and refine compliance-safe commercialization.

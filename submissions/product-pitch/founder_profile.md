# Founder Profile

## Sidnei Barbieri — Solo Founder

**One line:** I built ArenaWealth Pro to manage my own international equity
portfolio, then turned it into a product because the problem is everyone's.

---

## The Story

I invest in US equities through a Brazilian broker. Like most self-directed
investors, my workflow was a CSV export and a spreadsheet — no audit trail, no
data-source health, no guardrail against placing a trade so small the fee eats
the return.

So I built the tool I needed. ArenaWealth Pro is dogfooded on my real holdings.
Every feature exists because I hit the problem before I shipped the fix:

- I needed to import the newest broker export automatically → inbox auto-import.
- I needed to know if prices were live or stale → provider health semaphores.
- I almost placed a fee-eroded micro-order → the $250 economic guardrail.
- I wanted to know why the tool recommended something last week → the decision log.

---

## Why Solo Is a Strength Here

- **Speed:** One decision-maker, AI-leveraged development, fast iteration from
  real use to shipped feature.
- **Authentic demand:** I am the first user. The product is validated against a
  real portfolio every day, not a hypothetical persona.
- **Discipline:** The architecture is free-data-first and reproducible
  (`make verify` passes) — built to be inspected, not just demoed.

---

## Proof Points

- Free-data-first: runs with no paid API keys.
